# RetailNova Cybersecurity Risk Assessment (Case Study)

A cybersecurity risk assessment I wrote for **RetailNova Pty Ltd**, a Melbourne-based retailer used as a case study. This is not a real client engagement.

**Prepared by:** Jamal Bekkouche-Henry

**Report date:** 30 July 2026

## What it covers

- **Scope:** 85 stores with POS, a custom e-commerce site, a mobile app, Salesforce, SAP, AWS and third-party vendors
- **Assets and data:** inventory of key systems and data, rated by criticality and sensitivity
- **Threats:** organised crime, opportunistic attackers, insiders, supply-chain compromise and hacktivists, tied to the company's 2023-2025 incident history
- **Risk register:** 12 risks scored with a likelihood × impact model (2 Critical, 4 High, 5 Medium, 1 Low)
- **Recommendations:** prioritised actions over 0-3, 3-6 and 6-12 months, including MFA, ransomware-resilient backups, a PCI DSS gap assessment, a third-party risk process, CSPM for AWS and BEC controls
